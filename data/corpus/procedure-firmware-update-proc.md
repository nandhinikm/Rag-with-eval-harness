# Firmware Update Rollout Procedure

**Category:** procedure  
**Company:** Solara Robotics Inc.

Employees are encouraged to raise questions about this topic during team stand-ups. This information is considered internal and should not be shared outside the company.

This policy applies to all Solara Robotics employees, contractors, and interns unless otherwise noted. New firmware is rolled out to 5% of the fleet first, and only expanded fleet-wide after 72 hours with no reported faults. A summary of related changes is available in the engineering changelog.
