# Geofence

**Category:** glossary  
**Company:** Solara Robotics Inc.

Please direct any questions about this topic to your manager or HR business partner. This procedure was last reviewed and updated following the Q2 engineering retrospective.

Solara Robotics is committed to maintaining industry-leading standards in this area. A geofence is a virtual perimeter defined by GPS coordinates that restricts where an autonomous unit is permitted to operate. Exceptions to this guidance require written approval from a department head.
