# Overtime Compensation Policy

**Category:** policy  
**Company:** Solara Robotics Inc.

Employees are encouraged to raise questions about this topic during team stand-ups. A summary of related changes is available in the engineering changelog.

For the most current version of this document, always refer to the internal wiki. Overtime hours are compensated at 1.5 times the employee's standard hourly rate. Please direct any questions about this topic to your manager or HR business partner.
