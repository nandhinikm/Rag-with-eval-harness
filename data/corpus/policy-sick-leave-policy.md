# Sick Leave Policy

**Category:** policy  
**Company:** Solara Robotics Inc.

Solara Robotics is committed to maintaining industry-leading standards in this area. For the most current version of this document, always refer to the internal wiki.

This procedure was last reviewed and updated following the Q2 engineering retrospective. Employees accrue 10 paid sick days per year, which do not roll over to the next year. Exceptions to this guidance require written approval from a department head.
