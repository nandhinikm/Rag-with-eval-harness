# Bug Triage Procedure

**Category:** procedure  
**Company:** Solara Robotics Inc.

This procedure was last reviewed and updated following the Q2 engineering retrospective. This information is considered internal and should not be shared outside the company.

This document is maintained by the Product team and reviewed quarterly. Bugs labeled as severity-1 must be triaged by the engineering team within 2 hours of being filed. Employees are encouraged to raise questions about this topic during team stand-ups.
