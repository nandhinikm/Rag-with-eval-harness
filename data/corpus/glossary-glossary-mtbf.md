# MTBF

**Category:** glossary  
**Company:** Solara Robotics Inc.

Employees are encouraged to raise questions about this topic during team stand-ups. For the most current version of this document, always refer to the internal wiki.

Solara Robotics is committed to maintaining industry-leading standards in this area. MTBF (Mean Time Between Failures) is the average operating time between one hardware failure and the next for a given unit type. A summary of related changes is available in the engineering changelog.
