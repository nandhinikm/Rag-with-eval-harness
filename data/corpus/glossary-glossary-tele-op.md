# Tele-operation

**Category:** glossary  
**Company:** Solara Robotics Inc.

Employees are encouraged to raise questions about this topic during team stand-ups. Exceptions to this guidance require written approval from a department head.

This information is considered internal and should not be shared outside the company. Tele-operation is the direct remote control of a unit by a human operator, used when autonomous mode is unsafe or unavailable. A summary of related changes is available in the engineering changelog.
