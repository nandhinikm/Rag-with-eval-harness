# Remote Work Policy

**Category:** policy  
**Company:** Solara Robotics Inc.

This information is considered internal and should not be shared outside the company. This document is maintained by the Safety team and reviewed quarterly.

This policy applies to all Solara Robotics employees, contractors, and interns unless otherwise noted. Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week with manager approval. Please direct any questions about this topic to your manager or HR business partner.
