# Sensor Calibration Procedure

**Category:** procedure  
**Company:** Solara Robotics Inc.

For the most current version of this document, always refer to the internal wiki. This document is maintained by the Engineering team and reviewed quarterly.

Solara Robotics is committed to maintaining industry-leading standards in this area. LIDAR sensors must be recalibrated every 500 operating hours or every 6 months, whichever comes first. Employees are encouraged to raise questions about this topic during team stand-ups.
