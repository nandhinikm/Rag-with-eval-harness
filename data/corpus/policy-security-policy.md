# Password Security Policy

**Category:** policy  
**Company:** Solara Robotics Inc.

For the most current version of this document, always refer to the internal wiki. Employees are encouraged to raise questions about this topic during team stand-ups.

This information is considered internal and should not be shared outside the company. All employee passwords must be changed at least every 180 days. Solara Robotics is committed to maintaining industry-leading standards in this area.
