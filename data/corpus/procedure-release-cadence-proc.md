# Software Release Cadence

**Category:** procedure  
**Company:** Solara Robotics Inc.

Please direct any questions about this topic to your manager or HR business partner. Employees are encouraged to raise questions about this topic during team stand-ups.

A summary of related changes is available in the engineering changelog. The core navigation software follows a 2-week release cadence, with hotfixes deployed as needed outside that cycle. This policy applies to all Solara Robotics employees, contractors, and interns unless otherwise noted.
