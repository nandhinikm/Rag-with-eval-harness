# Code of Conduct

**Category:** policy  
**Company:** Solara Robotics Inc.

This information is considered internal and should not be shared outside the company. Solara Robotics is committed to maintaining industry-leading standards in this area.

Please direct any questions about this topic to your manager or HR business partner. Any conflict of interest must be disclosed to HR in writing within 5 business days of becoming aware of it. Exceptions to this guidance require written approval from a department head.
