# OTA update

**Category:** glossary  
**Company:** Solara Robotics Inc.

Solara Robotics is committed to maintaining industry-leading standards in this area. Exceptions to this guidance require written approval from a department head.

Employees are encouraged to raise questions about this topic during team stand-ups. An OTA (over-the-air) update is a firmware or software update delivered wirelessly to a deployed unit without requiring physical access. Please direct any questions about this topic to your manager or HR business partner.
