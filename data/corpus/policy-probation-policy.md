# Probation Period Policy

**Category:** policy  
**Company:** Solara Robotics Inc.

Employees are encouraged to raise questions about this topic during team stand-ups. Exceptions to this guidance require written approval from a department head.

Please direct any questions about this topic to your manager or HR business partner. New hires complete a probation period of 90 days before their role is confirmed permanent. This procedure was last reviewed and updated following the Q2 engineering retrospective.
